The Butterfly Atlas

A working notebook of butterflies spotted and pinned from travels around the world — each page noted with its species, the place it was found, and the day. Kept in the hope it's useful to fellow enthusiasts.

Photographs by Robert Godden

About the photographer

My interest in wildlife started with birds and butterflies in Kent, UK over 50 years ago. This has expanded to include many other groups of wildlife … and many other places! My photography is mostly limited to subjects that I can get close to. Nowadays I particularly enjoy watching and photographing butterflies, travelling to wonderful places to see them and the challenge of identifying unfamiliar species from the photographs.

I intend to add more albums for the UK, other European countries and Peru in coming months, so check again from time to time.
